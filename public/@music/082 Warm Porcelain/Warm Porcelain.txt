**(Verse 1)** 
I leave the flower shop and step through the night, 
To Parkstreet fourteen under yellow streetlight. 
The smell of fresh beeswax and tea in the air, 
I cross the clean threshold, escaping despair.

**(Chorus 1)** 
*Warm porcelain cup in the palm of my hand,* 
*At last there's a floor where I safely can stand.* 
*The frost in my muscles begins to melt down,* 
*I'm reclaiming my life in this disciplined town.*

**(Verse 2)** 
Mark offers his hand with a gentle, light frame, 
He steps with respect and he honors my name. 
At the big golden table with jasmine and steam, 
The old couple smiles as I wake from my dream.

**(Chorus 2)** 
*The Rumba flows through me like liquid hot fire,* 
*No longer a prey to some stranger's desire.* 
*With dignity, power, and boundaries clear,* 
*I'm owning the floor and I'm shedding my fear.*

**(Outro)** 
Warm porcelain tea and a partner who cares, 
No hands on my spine, no more predatory stares. 
The Rumba is mine and my spirit is free, 
Warm porcelain hands... bringing life back to me.
