[Verse 1]

Mary steps into the hall.

Silence gathers round our stall.

I have seen the way you fly.

Carving lightning through the sky.

[Chorus 1]

This small room will clip your wings.

Praise from fools and hollow things.

Do not let your hunger drown.

Break the borders of this town.

[Verse 2]

Look at me and hear my voice.

Greatness leaves you with no choice.

I can see the lion wake.

Feel the timber start to shake.

[Chorus 2]

Lotus calls you to the street.

Where the hungry fighters meet.

Leave this easy floor behind.

Sharpen every step you find.

[Verse 3]

Clasp her hand beneath the lace.

Look the future in the face.

I demand your sacred word.

Stronger than the whispers heard.

[Chorus 3]

Promise me you will not stay.

Choose the harder, higher way.

Burn the bridge and take the floor.

Mary’s school is yours no more.
