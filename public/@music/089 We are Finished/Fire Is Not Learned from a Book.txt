**(Verse 1)** 
I saw the heavy door slam shut. 
Ginger, drink this warm porcelain tea. 
Stop looking for a golden pin, 
On a suit you want to see.

**(Chorus 1)** 
*Fire isn't taught in books,* 
*Nor on a shiny badge.* 
*You look in all the wrong places,* 
*Standing right on the edge.*

**(Verse 2)** 
Look at the empty chair right here. 
Paul sat where you sit right now. 
He doesn't want to rule your heart, 
Or make your spirit bow.

**(Chorus 2)** 
*He doesn't want to tame your soul,* 
*Or pull you down with pride.* 
*He wants to stand inside your fire,* 
*And feel it by your side.*

**(Bridge)** 
You laugh and ask if I am mad, 
"That boy who stutters on his keys?" 
I say he looked at you with awe, 
Not looking for a prize to seize.

**(Outro)** 
Drink your tea, my weary child. 
Let the cold anger part. 
True fire isn't taught in **books**... 
It starts inside a true heart.
