[Verse 1]
The heavy exit door slams shut behind,
My black pumps discarded on the stones.
Cold autumn air hits my burning skin,
While our thunder still echoes in my bones.

[Chorus 1]
My hand finds the warmth along your face,
No steps to count, no crowded floor to share.
I am standing right here beyond the dance,
Waiting for the lion to strip me bare.

[Verse 2]
Pink lace clings wet against my chest,
Your breath still racing hard and deep.
You lifted me so high a while ago,
A promise that I thought we’d keep.

[Chorus 2]
My hand lingers soft against your cheek,
Begging you to cross this quiet line.
Must a queen bend down to plead with you,
To claim what was already yours and mine?

[Verse 3]
You smile that careful, polished smile,
Rebuilding the wall you broke tonight.
You praise the band and the steps we took,
Hiding away from the streetlamp light.

[Chorus 3]
My fingers slip slowly from your jaw,
As the fire turns to ash inside the dark.
You choose the safety of your cage,
Afraid of the fire you dared to spark.

[Verse 4]
I gather my heels from the frozen stones,
Straps hanging heavy in my hand.
I put my dancer's mask back on,
Giving you what you demand.

[Chorus 4]
I turn to the door and lock the gate,
Back to the rhythm and the polite refrain:
See you at the next lesson, Paul—
We will never dance this close again.
